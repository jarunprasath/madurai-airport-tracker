About this tracker

A community dashboard of air traffic at Madurai International Airport, built to give citizens, businesses and authorities clear evidence for more flights and better connectivity.

Where the numbers come from

  • Official passengers, aircraft movements and cargo: Airports Authority of India (AAI) monthly Traffic News, published on aai.aero, usually 4–8 weeks after month end.
  • Flight schedules, airlines, aircraft types and status: AeroDataBox flight data API.
  • Live aircraft positions: OpenSky Network (ADS-B, volunteer receivers).

Estimates – please read

  • AAI does not publish daily or per-flight passenger counts. Daily and weekly passenger numbers here are estimates: typical seats for each aircraft type × a load factor calibrated against AAI monthly totals (–).
  • Forecasts use the seasonal pattern of past years (festivals, school holidays) and the recent trend. They are statistical estimates, not official projections.
  • Data gaps happen when a source is unavailable. Coverage is shown with each figure.
